# MC1RANGER

**Threat Detection Engineering · Cyber Threat Intelligence · Threat Hunting**

I work at the intersection of adversary research and defensive engineering, turning threat intelligence and endpoint telemetry into practical hunting hypotheses and testable detections.

## Focus areas

- **Detection Engineering:** behavioral detection logic, validation, tuning, and analyst documentation.
- **Threat Hunting:** hypothesis-driven investigations informed by adversary TTPs and MITRE ATT&CK.
- **Cyber Threat Intelligence:** connecting indicators, infrastructure, malware behavior, and operational context.
- **SentinelOne:** hunting and detection workflows for endpoint telemetry.
- **Automation:** repeatable enrichment, triage, and research workflows.

## From intelligence to detection

```text
Threat intelligence → Hunt hypothesis → Telemetry → Detection → Validation → Tuning
```

Good detection content explains the behavior, required data, validation method, false positives, and coverage limitations.

## Research principles

- Prioritize adversary behavior and evidence.
- Document assumptions and map techniques to [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) where applicable.
- Validate in controlled environments before operational use.
- Share public, synthetic, or sanitized examples while protecting sensitive information.

## Collaboration

Research discussion, technical corrections, and validation results are welcome through issues and pull requests in the relevant repository.
